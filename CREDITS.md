# Data attribution

The local US/Canada OpenAddresses build uses data from the providers below.
OpenAddresses does not relicense their data; the source-specific terms apply.
Keep this file with any deployment using these sources. The downloaded
`data/openaddresses/<country>/*.source.json` files record the job and terms
used for each import.

- Canada National Address Register: Adapted from Statistics Canada, National Address Register, 2025-07-29. This does not constitute an endorsement by Statistics Canada of this product. [Terms](https://www.statcan.gc.ca/en/reference/licence).
- City of Calgary: Contains information licensed under the [Open Government Licence – City of Calgary](https://data.calgary.ca/stories/s/Open-Calgary-Terms-of-Use/u45n-7awa).
- City of Toronto: Contains information licensed under the [Open Government Licence – Toronto](https://www.toronto.ca/city-government/data-research-maps/open-data/open-data-licence/).
- New Mexico 911 (NM911) Program. [Source terms](https://gstore.unm.edu/apps/rgis/datasets/8850b25e-e495-46b5-952d-0fa1fa129ada/metadata/FGDC-STD-001-1998.html).
- Maine Emergency Services Communication Bureau. [Source terms](https://maine.hub.arcgis.com/datasets/27e5daf37f1f4e5c8231d6e3989835c4_0/about).
